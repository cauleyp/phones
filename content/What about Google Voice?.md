
It makes some sense to start here, and Google Voice has come a long way. Before it was account centric, meaning a number was tied to a specific account. That means accounts numbers were all on their own and we lose a lot of features.
- No transferring phone calls
- No auto attendant
- No night time hours
- No "main line" 
- No E911

It looks like this has changed and it may be a viable option as it seems to offer all of these features now. One thing that is odd about Google Voice for organizations is that Google does not really publicize how much it costs nor do they really advertise or push this service. 

## Physical phones
While other services are pretty flexible with what type of phones we can use, Google Voice is pretty restrictive. On their website, it says the only support [Polyphones produced by HP](https://www.hp.com/us-en/poly/phones/desk-phones.html). 

While I am sure these phones are fine to use, they may turn out to be more expensive and as opposed to more widely used phones. 

Google is very much an "online" phone system - meaning they want you to install the app on their phone and use your personal phone as your business phone. Whereas other systems may offer that as a feature, but prefer to have a work phone separate from a personal one.
